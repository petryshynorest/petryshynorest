# Hi, I'm Orest 👋

Founder & CEO of [Diagnelia](https://github.com/diagnelia), an AI guide for people still searching for a diagnosis. Based in Ivano-Frankivsk, Ukraine.

## What I'm building
People with rare conditions often wait years for a diagnosis. Diagnelia turns years of symptoms, visits and documents into one clear, sourced page that the next doctor can read in two minutes.
- **Stage 1, for patients:** a guided AI conversation, document reading and a one-page chronology in clinical terms. It organises information and never names a disease.
- **Stage 2, for clinicians:** decision support, to be developed as a medical device under the EU MDR.
